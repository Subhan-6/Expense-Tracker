# Privacy Policy — Expense Tracker

**Developer:** Muhammad Subhan Qamar  
**Contact email:** mazharsubhan69@gmail.com

## 1. Introduction

Expense Tracker helps users record expenses, manage budgets and income, organize spending categories, and review financial summaries. This Privacy Policy explains how the app stores, processes, and shares information when you use its features.

## 2. Information Stored on Your Device

Expense Tracker stores the financial information you enter, which may include:

- Expense names, amounts, dates, notes, and categories.
- Budget and income amounts.
- Custom expense categories and related preferences.
- Your optional AI savings coach API key.

Expense records are stored locally in the app's SQLite database. App preferences and the optional AI API key are stored locally using device storage. The app does not require you to create an account to use its core features.

Anyone who gains access to your unlocked device or app data may be able to access information stored locally.

## 3. Optional AI Savings Coach

If you choose to use the AI savings coach, the app sends aggregated expense-category totals and related financial summaries to Anthropic's API to generate personalized savings advice.

Expense titles and notes are not sent to the AI service by this feature. The AI feature is optional; you can use the app's core expense-tracking features without requesting AI advice.

To use this feature, you provide your own Anthropic API key. The key is stored locally on your device and transmitted to Anthropic when an AI request is made. Anthropic processes the request under its own privacy policy and applicable terms.

Review Anthropic's privacy information at https://www.anthropic.com/privacy.

## 4. How Information Is Used

Information stored by the app is used to:

- Record and display your expenses, income, and budgets.
- Calculate spending summaries, savings estimates, and financial insights.
- Provide category comparisons and budget-related suggestions.
- Generate AI savings advice when you explicitly request it.

The app's standard financial calculations are performed on your device.

## 5. Data Sharing

The developer does not operate a server that collects your expense records for this app. The app does not sell your personal information.

Financial summaries and your API key are transmitted to Anthropic only when you use the optional AI savings coach. Anthropic's handling of submitted information is governed by its own policies and terms.

The app does not include advertising or third-party advertising trackers.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your locally stored financial records remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app's data. Uninstalling the app may delete locally stored information. We recommend exporting or otherwise preserving important information before uninstalling or resetting the device, if such options are available.

Requests made to Anthropic may be subject to Anthropic's own data-retention practices and terms.

## 7. Data Security

The app uses local device storage for its core records. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Protect your device and keep your operating system up to date.

You are responsible for keeping your Anthropic API key confidential and monitoring any charges associated with its use.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Expense Tracker is a general-purpose financial utility and is not specifically designed for children. The developer does not knowingly collect children's personal information through a developer-operated server.

## 9. Your Choices

You may choose not to use the AI savings coach. You can manage or delete locally stored records through the app's available features and device settings.

You may also choose not to provide an Anthropic API key.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at the privacy-policy URL linked on the app's Google Play listing.
